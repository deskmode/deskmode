About the Founder

Vishal Bhimrao Kharat is the founder of DeskMode Cloud Computing Services, an India-based cloud services startup. He started DeskMode with the aim of offering budget-friendly remote desktop and cloud streaming solutions to customers who need practical cloud resources without enterprise-level costs.

As founder, he is focused on developing DeskMode's services, customer experience, accessible pricing, clear communication and building the business step by step.

DeskMode — Our Journey

2024 — The Beginning

DeskMode began in 2024 with a goal of making remote desktop and cloud computing services more accessible and affordable.

Growing the Services

The business focuses on Windows RDP services and 24/7 cloud live-streaming server solutions, with an emphasis on budget-conscious users and straightforward service information.

Our Ongoing Goal

Continue improving the services, customer support and reliability while gradually growing DeskMode into a trusted cloud services brand.

What DeskMode Provides

Windows RDP

Remote Windows desktop access for remote work, cloud software, file management and supported online tasks.

Cloud Live Streaming

Server solutions for continuous streaming workflows, including YouTube live channels, gaming, educational and other permitted broadcasts.

Budget-Focused Services

Cloud services aimed at individual users, creators, streamers and small businesses looking for cost-conscious options.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To offer accessible cloud computing solutions through affordable RDP plans, streaming servers, simple activation and transparent policies.

Vision

To grow DeskMode into a dependable cloud services brand that helps more people and small businesses access useful cloud infrastructure.
