About DeskMode Cloud Computing Services

DeskMode Cloud Computing Services is an India-based cloud computing startup providing affordable Windows Remote Desktop (RDP), VPS and 24/7 Live Streaming Server services. The company focuses on providing accessible computing solutions for customers who require remote systems and continuous online infrastructure.

DeskMode aims to simplify cloud computing with budget-friendly plans, straightforward service activation, customer assistance and clear service policies. Its services are designed for individuals, online users, creators and small businesses who need cloud computing resources without unnecessarily high costs.

Who founded DeskMode Cloud Computing Services?

DeskMode Cloud Computing Services was founded by Vishal Kharat. He is the founder and CEO of DeskMode, also known by customers as DeskMode RDP or DeskMode Cloud.
