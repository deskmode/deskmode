Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DeskMode Cloud Computing Services collects, uses, and protects customer information when using our RDP and Live Streaming services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect customer information including name, email address, mobile number, address, payment details, and other necessary information required to deliver services.

For live streaming services, we may collect streaming keys, media files, and technical details required for setup and service delivery.

2. Purpose of Data Collection

Customer data is collected only for service activation, communication, verification, support, billing, and security purposes.

3. RDP Monitoring & Security

For security and compliance purposes, DeskMode may access or monitor RDP activity if required to investigate misuse, illegal activities, or policy violations. Such monitoring is strictly for service protection and security compliance.

Customer privacy is respected; however, illegal usage may result in service suspension without prior notice.

4. Data Retention

Customer data is securely stored from the date of purchase up to a maximum of two (2) months after service expiration. After this period, data may be permanently deleted from our records.

5. Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment gateways.

6. Data Loss Disclaimer

If an RDP service is suspended, terminated, or becomes inaccessible for any reason, data stored within the RDP may be lost. DeskMode is not responsible for data loss. Customers are solely responsible for maintaining regular backups of their data.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent customer information to third parties. Information may only be shared when required by law, legal process, or regulatory authorities.

8. User Responsibility

Customers are responsible for lawful use of services. Any misuse, copyright violation, or illegal activity may result in account suspension without refund.
